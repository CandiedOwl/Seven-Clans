## Your people

Everyone in the clans is **human**. Other 5e species don't exist as peoples here, but two of them can be played as unusual humans, using their rules with a human look.

| Play as | Rules from | What it is in the world |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Human** | Human (2014: standard or variant with a feat; 2024: Human) | The default. Every clan is human. |
| **Stone-Blessed** | Goliath | Unusually tall, heavy humans, said to carry some of the Stone-Mother's weight. Most are born to the Auroch or in the highlands, but any clan can have one. Respected and a little feared. *2024 rules:* choose Stone's Endurance, Hill's Tumble or Frost's Chill as your giant ancestry; the others are too flashy. |
| **Unbowed** | Orc (2024) or Half-Orc (2014) | Humans born with a fury that won't let them fall: Relentless Endurance. Most common among the Boar. They look human; their darkvision is simply sharp night-eyes. |

**Not playable:**
- **Elves, dwarves, halflings, gnomes, dragonborn, tieflings and aasimar:** these peoples don't exist.
- **Animal-folk** (such as tabaxi, kenku, tortles or lizardfolk): the clans honor their animals; they are not animals. A creature that is half beast is a spirit.
- **Half-spirits:** they exist, but are too rare and strange to play.

If you want a strange birth, play a human and take **Spirit-Touched** (Sorcerer; see [the spirit-touched](@/customs-and-beliefs.md#the-spirit-touched)), or choose to be **clanless** ([below](@/making-a-character.md#your-clan)).

## Your clan

Choose one of the seven clans ([The Clans](@/the-clans.md)). Your clan gives you:

| Clan | Bonus skill | Taboo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wolf | Survival | Never kill a wolf or eat wolf meat |
| Raven | Deception | Always leave part of every kill for the ravens |
| Seal | Athletics (swimming) | Never kill a seal |
| Owl | Perception | Never kill an owl, and never imitate its call |
| Auroch | Athletics (wrestling) | Never kill a herd's lead bull; never leave horns on the plain |
| Boar | Intimidation | Never run from a charging boar |
| Reindeer | Animal Handling | Never kill the lead doe; lay reindeer bones facing the herd's path |

You may also choose to be **clanless**. You have no Guardian, no bonus skill and no clan taboo. You are harder for spirits to read, and you are often mistrusted.

### Guardian's Favor

- **Once per long rest,** if you have kept your taboos, you can call on your Guardian for **advantage on one roll** that fits your clan's creature.
- **If you break a taboo,** you lose Guardian's Favor until you atone with a ritual, an offering or a quest. Each broken taboo also makes you easier for dark spirits to reach.

## Languages

Every character knows **Fire-Speech**, their **clan's tongue**, and **one more** language from the table below. Under the 2024 rules this is the usual "Common plus two". Under the 2014 rules it replaces the languages a species or background would give.

- **Clanless** characters know Fire-Speech and **any two** other languages. They've drifted between camps.
- **The First Tongue** needs the GM's agreement, and is mostly for Spirit-Talkers, Skin-Changers and Wise-Ones.

### Spoken languages

| Language | Spoken by | What it's like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fire-Speech** | Everyone | The shared tongue of gatherings, trade and councils, spoken around the common fire. Plain and practical, with few words for anything sacred. *(Replaces Common.)* |
| **Wolf-tongue** | [Wolf](@/people-of-the-wolf.md) | Low and clipped, with much left unsaid. Hunters drop half their words and finish sentences with a look. Close kin to Boar-tongue. |
| **Boar-tongue** | [Boar](@/people-of-the-boar.md) | Loud, blunt and boastful, full of oaths. Wolf and Boar can follow each other's speech, and both hate admitting it. |
| **Raven-tongue** | [Raven](@/people-of-the-raven.md) | A river-traders' tongue full of borrowed words, puns and double meanings. A Raven speaker can say two things at once, and often does. |
| **Seal-tongue** | [Seal](@/people-of-the-seal.md) | Rhythmic and songlike, shaped by rowing chants and net-songs. Many words for water, ice and weather. Close kin to Reindeer-tongue. |
| **Reindeer-tongue** | [Reindeer](@/people-of-the-reindeer.md) | Slow and patient, with words for every kind of snow, track and sky. Seal and Reindeer speakers understand each other well, one reason the two clans are allies. |
| **Owl-tongue** | Owl | Soft and old-fashioned, spoken in murmurs. It keeps many words from the First Tongue, and it changes least of all the clan tongues. |
| **Auroch-tongue** | [Auroch](@/people-of-the-auroch.md) | Formal and proud, with titles, honors and ritual boasts. It is related to no other clan's tongue, and the Auroch like it that way. |

**Kin tongues:** someone who speaks Wolf-tongue can follow simple Boar-tongue, and the other way round. The same goes for Seal-tongue and Reindeer-tongue. Anything subtle, fast or full of slang needs an Intelligence check.

### Special languages

| Language | Who knows it | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The First Tongue** | Spirits; fragments known by spirit-workers | The speech from the time when humans, animals and spirits could speak freely ([Spirits of the World](@/spirits-of-the-world.md)). Great spirits, Guardians and place-spirits speak it. No living person speaks it fluently, and every rite uses scraps of it. *(Replaces Primordial, Sylvan, Celestial, Infernal and Abyssal.)* |
| **Hunt-Hands** | Hunters of every clan | A silent hand-language for the hunt: game, numbers, directions, danger. Hunters from rival clans can understand each other with it. It has no words for anything that isn't hunting or fighting. |
| **Trail-signs** | Trail-Ghosts | Marks scratched on bark and stone that only initiates can read. *(Replaces Thieves' Cant.)* |
| **Skin-Speech** | Skin-Changers only | The Skin-Changers' secret tongue: calls, clicks and gestures learned from animals and spirits. Teaching it to an outsider is taboo. *(Replaces Druidic.)* |
| **Knot-Cord** | [Owl](@/people-of-the-owl.md) record-keepers, Bone-Readers | The only real writing in the world: knots tied in cords and marks carved on bone, kept in the Bone Libraries. Most people can't read at all. |

### What doesn't exist

Elvish, Dwarvish, Halfling, Gnomish, Draconic, Giant, Goblin, Orc, Undercommon and Deep Speech don't exist here. A Stone-Blessed or Unbowed character takes their clan's tongue instead of Giant or Orc. Monsters that would speak one of these speak the First Tongue if they are spirits, and nothing at all if they are beasts.

Writing isn't common. Beyond Knot-Cord, people use [clan marks](@/customs-and-beliefs.md#the-clan-mark), pack marks and trail-signs, which are signs, not language.

## Classes

Characters start at **3rd level**, and this campaign runs to **6th level**.

Every power in this world comes from a **relationship**: with spirits, ancestors, a Guardian, a taboo, or your own body and breath. Magic is never a formula, and no gods grant spells.

- **Hunter** (Ranger or Fighter): tracking, survival, and the bow or spear. Little or no open spellcasting, but ritual techniques such as reading terrain and speaking to animals.
  *Ranger subclasses:* Hunter → Prey-Hunter · Beast Master → Beast-Bonded · Gloom Stalker → Dusk-Stalker · Fey Wanderer → Mist-Wanderer (the River-of-Mists) · Swarmkeeper → Swarm-Kin
- **Skin-Changer** (Druid): animal forms, herbs, and weather and land magic, always framed as bargaining with spirits.
  *Subclasses:* Moon → Many-Skinned · Land → Rooted (bound to one region's spirits) · Shepherd → Spirit-Herder · Dreams → Dream-Walker (Owl's dream-travel) · Spores → Rot-Walker (risky; decay is close to twisting) · **new:** [Circle of the Clan-Skin](@/custom-subclasses.md#circle-of-the-clan-skin-skin-changer)
- **Spirit-Talker** (Cleric or Warlock): speaks with ancestors, totems or one great spirit. Visions, protective charms, curses. A Warlock-style Spirit-Talker holds a pact with one particular spirit, and pacts are how spirit-twisters begin, so take care.
  *Cleric subclasses:* Life → Mender · Grave → Ancestor-Speaker · Nature → Green-Speaker · Tempest → Storm-Speaker (the Whale Ancestor's storms) · Trickery → Mist-Speaker (Raven crossings) · **new:** [Soul-Tender](@/custom-subclasses.md#soul-tender-spirit-talker)
  *Warlock subclasses:* Archfey → Wild-Bound (a forest or trickster spirit) · Fathomless → Sea-Bound · Undead → Grave-Bound (a dead ancestor) · Great Old One → Deep-Bound (dangerous: something old and unnamed) · Hexblade → Edge-Bound (a spirit living in an obsidian blade)
- **Wise-One** (Bard): keeper of lore, stories and songs that bind spirits, or soothe or inflame them. Magic as story-power and ritual chant, not flashy spells.
  *Subclasses:* Lore → Lore-Keeper · Spirits → Spirit-Teller · Valor → War-Chanter · Eloquence → Council-Voice · Whispers → Whisper-Tongue (secrets; risky)
- **Blooded Warrior** (Fighter or Barbarian): trance, battle-fury and ancestral blessings, without formal spells.
  *Fighter subclasses:* Champion → First Spear · Battle Master → Hunt-Master · Banneret → War-Leader (a clan war-chief) · Samurai → Unyielding (Boar's "face it or fall")
  *Barbarian subclasses:* Totem Warrior / Wild Heart → Totem-Blooded · Ancestral Guardian → Ancestor-Shielded · Berserker → Blood-Fury · Beast → Fang-Born · **new:** [Taboo-Bearer](@/custom-subclasses.md#taboo-bearer-blooded-warrior)
- **Trail-Ghost** (Rogue): scouts, trappers, spies and thieves, living by skill and silence. Instead of Thieves' Cant you know **trail-signs**, marks scratched on bark and stone that only initiates can read.
  *Subclasses:* Thief → Scavenger · Scout → Trail-Ghost · Assassin → Quiet Knife · Arcane Trickster → Trickster-Bound (bargains with small trickster spirits)
- **Spirit-Touched** (Sorcerer): power you were born with, from an omen at your birth or a survival that should have killed you. Revered and feared ([the spirit-touched](@/customs-and-beliefs.md#the-spirit-touched)).
  *Subclasses:* Draconic → Star-Blooded (born under a falling-star omen; veins that glow) · Wild Magic → Star-Touched · Storm → Storm-Born · Divine Soul → Guardian-Chosen · Shadow → Dark-Touched (dangerous; close to twisting)
- **Still-One** (Monk): fasting, breath and trance, like Owl's Silent Watch and Wolf's Silent Walks. Your ki is **spirit-breath**, and your body is the weapon when you have nothing else.
  *Subclasses:* Open Hand → Way of the Still Breath · Shadow → Night-Walker · Long Death → Bone-Watcher · Drunken Master → Thorn-Fire Dancer
- **Oath-Sworn** (Paladin): a champion sworn to a Guardian or a great spirit. **Your oath is a set of taboos.** Divine Smite is the **Guardian's Wrath**, and Lay on Hands is your Guardian's healing touch.
  *Subclasses:* Devotion → Sworn to the Clan · Ancients → Sworn to the World-Tree · Vengeance → Hunter of Twisters
- **Bone-Reader** (Wizard, Owl only, rare): magic learned from the Bone Libraries and knotted cords. Your spellbook is a **string of carved bones**, which can be stolen, broken or forgotten.

Smiths who can work star-iron, the **Iron-Singers**, are too rare to play. Their work is something you may earn in the world.

## Spells

Characters only reach 6th level, so only spells of 1st to 3rd level matter. The banned spells below are the flashiest of those: raw magic, metal, and anything that makes the wild safe. Everything else is allowed, but describe it as a spirit's help, a bargain or a ritual, not a formula.

### Banned spells

| Spell | Level | Why it's banned |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Chaos Bolt*, *Chromatic Orb*, *Magic Missile* | 1st | Raw magical force. Power here always comes from a spirit, never from nowhere. |
| *Silvery Barbs*, *Tenser's Floating Disk* | 1st | Spell-formula tricks with no spirit behind them. |
| *Scorching Ray*, *Melf's Acid Arrow*, *Aganazzar's Scorcher*, *Dragon's Breath* | 2nd | Arcane blasts. There are no dragons. |
| *Cloud of Daggers*, *Shadow Blade* | 2nd | Metal blades and swords don't exist. |
| *Continual Flame* | 2nd | Fire must be fed and tended. Endless light would undo [the fear of the dark](@/customs-and-beliefs.md#fire-and-the-edge-of-the-dark). |
| *Rope Trick* | 2nd | There are no hidden pockets between worlds to hide in. |
| *Arcane Lock*, *Magic Mouth*, *Nystul's Magic Aura* | 2nd | Wizards' tricks with no spirit behind them. |
| *Fireball*, *Lightning Bolt*, *Melf's Minute Meteors* | 3rd | The flashiest blasts. Falling stars are sacred, not ammunition. |
| *Leomund's Tiny Hut* | 3rd | A magic shelter would take the danger out of the night. |
| *Fly* | 3rd | Only spirits, and Skin-Changers in animal form, take to the sky. |
| *Tiny Servant* | 3rd | No crafted servants. |
| *Animate Dead* | 3rd | Raising the dead breaks the deepest taboo. It is a [spirit-twister](@/customs-and-beliefs.md#spirit-twisters)'s crime. |

### Allowed, with a reskin

- *Mage Armor:* a spirit-hide settles over you.
- *Shield:* your Guardian turns the blow aside.
- *Misty Step:* a step through the River-of-Mists.
- *Find Familiar:* a small spirit in animal shape.
- *Unseen Servant:* a minor hearth-spirit doing your bidding.
- *Glyph of Warding:* a spirit-mark carved into wood, bone or stone.
- *Spiritual Weapon:* an ancestor's spear, fighting beside you.
- *Counterspell* and *Dispel Magic:* breaking a spirit's bargain, or talking it out of its work.
- *Call Lightning:* the sky spirits' anger, called down.

A great spirit may, rarely, grant a banned spell as a gift. That is the GM's call, and it always comes at a price.

## Gear and metal

- **Materials:** flint, obsidian, bone, antler, wood, hide and sinew. There is **no steel or bronze**.
- **Metal:** native copper is a rare trade treasure. **Star-iron**, from the Ember-Heart Crater, is the only true metal: sacred, dangerous, and never ordinary gear.
- **Weapons:** spears, javelins, bows, slings, clubs, and flint or obsidian axes and knives, with their normal stats. There are no swords.
- **Armor:**
  - **Light:** hide and fur
  - **Medium:** bone or antler lamellar; layered sea-hide (Seal)
  - **Heavy (rare):** whale-bone plate (Seal); layered auroch-hide (Auroch)
  - **Shields:** wicker and hide
- **Wealth:** there are no coins; everything is barter. **Shell wealth** (which the Seal control), amber, good flint, furs, and favors owed.
- **Optional rule:** flint and obsidian weapons chip on a natural 1 and need resharpening.

## Questions to answer

- What does your clan think of the clan of the person sitting next to you, and do *you* secretly agree or disagree?
- What rumor about another clan did you grow up believing? ("Everyone knows Raven can't be trusted with cattle." "Boar don't respect forest taboos.")
- Which taboo do you keep closest to your heart?
- Which rite of passage have you passed, and how did it go?
- If you were sent to a great council of the clans: did you volunteer, or were you chosen? Why?
