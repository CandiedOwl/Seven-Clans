Three subclasses made for this world, one for each of its oldest ideas: taboos, the three souls, and the clan-creature. They are built for levels 3 to 6, the range this campaign plays in. Talk to your GM before taking one; they are new and may be adjusted after play.

- [Taboo-Bearer](@/custom-subclasses.md#taboo-bearer-blooded-warrior) (Blooded Warrior, Barbarian)
- [Soul-Tender](@/custom-subclasses.md#soul-tender-spirit-talker) (Spirit-Talker, Cleric)
- [Circle of the Clan-Skin](@/custom-subclasses.md#circle-of-the-clan-skin-skin-changer) (Skin-Changer, Druid)

## Taboo-Bearer (Blooded Warrior)

Every clan keeps the taboos of its Guardian. A Taboo-Bearer takes on **more** of them. Each extra taboo is a weight carried for the clan and an agreement with the spirits, and the spirits pay for it in strength. People respect Taboo-Bearers and keep their distance. A Taboo-Bearer who breaks one of their taboos doesn't just lose favor. Everyone can see it.

The rules follow [Guardian's Favor](@/making-a-character.md) and the idea in [Taboos and balance](@/customs-and-beliefs.md#taboos-and-balance): a broken taboo is a danger, not a sin.

### 3rd level: Borne Taboo

Choose one **personal taboo** from the table below, or make one with the GM. You keep it on top of your clan taboo. Each taboo grants a **boon** that works while you rage, as long as you have kept **all** your taboos (clan and personal).

| Taboo | You must never… | Boon while raging |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Open Sky** | sleep under a roof, a tent or a cave ceiling | You can't be frightened, and allies within 10 feet of you have advantage on saves against being frightened. |
| **The Kept Name** | speak your own name aloud, or let it be carved or knotted | Resistance to psychic damage, and advantage on saves against being charmed. |
| **The Cold Hand** | touch fire, or eat food you cooked yourself | Resistance to cold and fire damage. |
| **The Unturned Back** | turn away from a foe who has struck you, until it falls or yields | Your speed rises by 10 feet when you move toward a hostile creature, and opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage. *(Suits Boar.)* |
| **The Given Word** | break a promise, even one made to an enemy | When a creature within 5 feet of you hits an ally, you can use your reaction to make one melee attack against it. |
| **The Unspoken Dead** | speak the name of someone who has died | Your weapon attacks against undead and spirits deal an extra 1d6 radiant damage. |

**Taboo-Sense.** With a 10-minute ritual, touching a willing or helpless creature, you learn whether it carries a broken taboo it has not atoned for. You don't learn which taboo, or how many.

### 6th level: Second Taboo

Choose a second personal taboo. You gain its boon too.

**Break the Bond.** While raging, you can use a bonus action to **break one of your personal taboos on purpose**. Until your rage ends, your rage damage bonus doubles and you have resistance to all damage except psychic. When the rage ends, you gain one level of exhaustion, and you lose that taboo's boon and your Guardian's Favor until you atone.

### Breaking a taboo by accident

If you break a personal taboo without meaning to, or under compulsion, you lose that taboo's boon and your Guardian's Favor until you atone. Your other boons keep working. The usual rule also applies: each broken taboo makes you easier for dark spirits to reach.

## Soul-Tender (Spirit-Talker)

Everyone has three souls: a **name-soul**, a **clan-soul** that belongs to their Guardian, and a **world-soul** ([Spirits of the World](@/spirits-of-the-world.md#the-three-souls)). Most Spirit-Talkers speak to spirits outside the body. A Soul-Tender looks after the spirits *inside* it. They are called to sickbeds, to people who wake up wrong after a fever, and to hunters who come back from the dark not quite themselves. Their work is to see what's frayed and call it home.

### Domain spells

You always have these spells prepared, and they don't count against the number you can prepare.

| Cleric level | Spells |
| --- | --- |
| 3rd | *protection from evil and good*, *sanctuary*, *calm emotions*, *gentle repose* |
| 5th | *speak with dead*, *remove curse* |

Flavor: *gentle repose* holds the souls of the dead near the body until the rites are done; *remove curse* untangles a spirit's grip.

### 3rd level: Three-Soul Sight

As an action, you look at a creature you can see within 10 feet and read its three souls. You learn:
- whether it is charmed, possessed, or under a curse;
- whether any of its souls is **frayed**, and which one: name, clan or world.

You don't learn what caused it. You can use this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all uses after a long rest.

### 3rd level: Channel Divinity: Call the Soul Home

As an action, you speak the names of up to three creatures you can see within 30 feet. Each one can end one effect causing it to be charmed or frightened, and gains temporary hit points equal to 5 + your cleric level. A possessed creature makes a new save against the possession, with advantage.

### 6th level: Bind the Souls

When a creature you can see within 30 feet makes a save against being charmed, frightened or possessed, or takes psychic or necrotic damage, you can use your reaction to hold its souls in place. It has advantage on the save, or the damage is halved. You can use this a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum once), and you regain all uses after a long rest.

## Circle of the Clan-Skin (Skin-Changer)

Every clan member wears a strip of their **clan-skin**: fur, feathers or hide from their clan-creature ([Spirits of the World](@/spirits-of-the-world.md#clan-guardians)). Druids of this circle wear the whole of it. They don't become an animal the way other Skin-Changers do. They keep their own body and take on their Guardian's: fangs, feathers, horns, a seal's sleek hide. They're less flexible than other shapeshifters and much closer to their clan.

A clanless character can't join this circle: they have no clan-creature to wear.

### 3rd level: Wear the Clan-Skin

As a bonus action, you can spend a use of Wild Shape to wear your clan-skin instead of changing shape. It lasts 10 minutes, until you are incapacitated, or until you end it as a bonus action. While you wear it:

- You keep your own statistics and can cast spells normally.
- You gain temporary hit points equal to your druid level + your Wisdom modifier.
- You grow **natural weapons** (fangs, claws, beak, horns or tusks). You can use Wisdom instead of Strength for their attack and damage rolls. They deal 1d8 piercing or slashing damage (bludgeoning for Auroch).
- You gain your clan's **aspect**:

| Clan | Aspect |
| --- | --- |
| **Wolf** | Your speed rises by 10 feet. Once per turn, when you hit a creature that one of your allies is within 5 feet of, it takes an extra 1d6 damage. |
| **Raven** | You can mimic any voice or sound you have heard. You take no damage from falling, and can glide 2 feet sideways for every 1 foot you fall. |
| **Seal** | You gain a swim speed equal to your walking speed, can hold your breath for an hour, and have resistance to cold damage. |
| **Owl** | You gain darkvision out to 60 feet (or 60 more feet), and advantage on Stealth checks. Creatures you hit can't make opportunity attacks against you until your next turn. |
| **Auroch** | If you move at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and hit it with your horns on the same turn, it must make a Strength save (your spell save DC) or fall prone. You have advantage on saves against being pushed or knocked prone. |
| **Boar** | If you move at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and hit it with your tusks, it takes an extra 1d6 damage. Once per wearing, if you drop to 0 hit points, you drop to 1 instead. |
| **Reindeer** | Your speed rises by 10 feet, you ignore difficult terrain caused by snow and ice, and you have resistance to cold damage. |

### 6th level: Kin-Call

While you wear your clan-skin, your natural weapons count as magical. Once per long rest, while wearing it, you can cast *conjure animals* without spending a spell slot. The spirits always appear as your clan-creature: a wolf pack, a cloud of ravens, a pod of seals, a hunting flight of owls, an auroch herd, a sounder of boar, or a reindeer herd.
