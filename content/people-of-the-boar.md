**[Clan mark](@/customs-and-beliefs.md#the-clan-mark).** **The slashes:** a ring cut through by two sets of three claw-strokes. It's scarred or tattooed on the arms and cheeks, and the Tuskrune adds a mark for every fight survived, so an old warrior's arms are covered in them.

**Home:** the scrub and marsh edges of the south-west, around the Mist-Bound Marshes ([The Land](@/the-land.md)).

The Boar live in dense thickets, marsh edges and broken scrub: the tangled, in-between lands where others fear to tread. They are hot-blooded, impulsive and stubborn. They prize ferocity and fearlessness, and believe the Boar spirit loves those who rush forward in spite of fear. Their rituals are loud: drums, shouting, stamping, painted faces.

They often raid for what they need instead of carefully managing it, which makes them enemies. But when an outside threat comes, there are no fiercer defenders.

## Their Guardian: Ember-Tusk

A wild, burning spirit of every boar that ever charged without fear: a massive boar with bristles like sparks and tusks glowing at the tips like cooling star-iron. It answers to the **Fallen Star-Spirit**. Its seat is the great bonfires of the Thorn-Fire Line, and it is fed by the **Breaking of Spears**.

**Taboo:** never run from a charging boar. Face it or fall.

## Leaders

- The chief is often just called **Big Tusks**, and the title can change hands by challenge.
- The war leader and the chief are often the same person.
- The spirit-talker is listened to only when times are clearly cursed. Then everyone listens.

## Traditions

- **Boar Runs:** wild night festivals of racing through dense undergrowth with only torches, shouting and laughing, daring each other not to flinch.
- **The Breaking of Spears:** after a great battle, damaged spear shafts are broken and burned, feeding the Boar spirit with "anger spent."
- **Scar Stories:** every scar has a tale, usually exaggerated. Evenings around the fire become contests for the best one.

## Rites of passage

- **The Bristle Test:** a child must touch the bristles of a penned young boar with bare hands without flinching. Those who retreat are teased kindly and try again next season.
- **Charge of the Young Tusks** (adulthood): join a raid or great hunt and stand in the front line at least once. Coming back without a single visible wound is suspicious.
- **The Tuskrune** (warrior marks): tusk-shaped marks cut or tattooed on the arms or cheeks, one for each great fight. Those with many are treated almost as walking legends.

## Families

- **The Mudroot Cluster:** a large kin-group who control many marsh trails. They can guide outsiders through the swamps, or mislead them. **Uron Mudroot**, a jovial but dangerous elder, loves testing strangers.
- **The Spine-Breakers:** infamous raiders, notorious among the Wolf and Auroch. Their leader is **Kessa Spine-Breaker**.
- **The Thorn-Fire Line:** fire-keepers and cooks. Their bonfires are legendary, and they brew powerful herbs and drink. A Thorn-Fire gathering can host tense diplomacy, or a brawl.

**Iron-Singer Nara**, a rare smith-shaman who speaks to stone and metal, is Boar. She works the star-iron of the Ember-Heart Crater.

## What the Boar say about others

- **Auroch:** brave, and good for a fight.
- **Seal:** we've hunted and raided the coasts together.
- **Reindeer:** they think we're crude.
- **Wolf:** they fuss over every tree in their forest. An old feud.
- **Owl:** we don't trust their whisper-magic.
