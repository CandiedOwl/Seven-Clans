## The Mistwater

The great river, and the thread through the heart of the land. It rises in the Ashen Highlands, runs below the Whispering Ravines and along the southern edge of the Deep Wolfwood, divides the Long Grass from the Boar scrub, and empties through the Mist-Bound Marshes into the sea. Only the far north, the Ice Sea coast and the White Reach, is off its path. Clans meet, trade and clash along its banks. The Raven hold its crossings. The Wolf River-Den camp trades on it, and the Creekperchers run a ferry nearby.

## The Deep Wolfwood

An immense old forest of towering black pines, moss-covered stones and misty ravines in the middle of the land. Sunlight is dim, and at night spirits and beasts rule. The Wolf live in its heart.

- **The Howling Stones:** a circle of jagged standing stones on a forest hilltop. When the wind blows, they howl like wolves. They are said to be the teeth of a long-dead forest spirit, and on certain nights the Great Wolf's shadow paces the circle.
- **The Root-Deep Cave:** a narrow crack in the earth under a great pine whose roots hang like curtains. Inside are twisting tunnels and pools lit by ghostly fungi. Children are told never to follow the little lights.

## The Whispering Ravines

Deep, misty ravines and cliffs where the Wolfwood climbs into the Ashen Highlands. Owls nest in the cliffs, and the wind in the gorges sounds like murmuring. The Owl live here and keep their Bone Libraries in the cliff caves.

## The Shattered Ice Sea

A bleak northern coast where the sea is choked with broken ice and black cliffs loom over the waves. Storm-spirits ride the gales, and whales are revered as wise elders. The Seal coast runs from here down the west side of the land.

- **The Whale-Bone Village:** a Seal village built inside the ribcages of colossal whale skeletons. Charms of shell and driftwood clatter in the wind. Its people swear by the tides and believe lies cause storms.
- **The Ice-Hunger Rift:** a jagged chasm in the sea ice that breathes cold mist and whispers. No one goes near it willingly.

## The White Reach

Tundra and taiga to the north-east, inland of the Ice Sea: snow-plains without end, low birch and pine at the southern edge, and skies full of aurora. The Reindeer follow their herds across it. The Frostveil Camp keeps to the southern edge, where outsiders meet them.

## The Long Grass

Rolling grasslands and river valleys south of the Wolfwood, running down to the southern coast and rising eastward into the Highland foothills. Great herds of wild cattle move across it. The Auroch claim it, mark it with boundary stones, and walk its edges every season.

## The Ashen Highlands

Broken mountains and volcanic plains to the east, with hot springs and geysers. The land steams and seethes, and fire and earth spirits mingle. The Mistwater rises here. No clan claims the Highlands.

- **The Steam-Blood Springs:** hot springs that heal, and can harm. The water runs red with minerals. This is **neutral ground**, where the clans hold their councils and rites of purification.
- **The Ember-Heart Crater:** a vast crater where a star fell long ago. The soil is black, laced with glowing red seams, and scattered with shards of **star-iron**. Strange beasts are reported near it.

## The Mist-Bound Marshes

A sprawling marsh in the south-west, where the Mistwater meets the sea. Reeds whisper, lights dance over still pools, and the line between the living and the dead is thin. The Boar live in the scrub and along its edges, and the Mudroot Cluster know its trails.

- **The Ancestors' Crossing:** a gnarled tree whose roots form a bridge over black, still water. On certain nights the ancestors walk here. People leave offerings to ask the dead for guidance or forgiveness.
- **The Sunken Longhouse:** an old clan hall, half-sunk in the marsh and leaning at a strange angle. It is said to stand half in this world and half in the spirit world, and that time runs strangely inside. No one remembers whose hall it was.
