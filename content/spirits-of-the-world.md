The clans see the unseen in layers:

- **The World Spirit:** a great, distant unity of mountain, sea, forest and sky. Few ever touch it. The oldest pacts rest on it.
- **The great spirits:** powerful beings tied to great things, such as the Great Wolf, the Whale Ancestor and the Stone-Mother.
- **Clan Guardians:** the spirit of each clan's creature.
- **Place-spirits:** smaller guardians of a river bend, a grove, a cave, a calving ground.
- **Ancestor spirits:** the dead, who can still advise, warn or mislead.

Every clan has stories of a time when people, animals and spirits could speak more freely, and of how those bonds were damaged.

## The great spirits

**The Great Wolf of the Deep Forest.** Spirit of wild packs, oaths and the balance between hunter and prey. It rules the Deep Wolfwood. On certain nights its shadow paces the Howling Stones: a massive wolf of shadow and starlight with eyes like twin moons. It punishes senseless killing and cowardice, and blesses brave hunters and loyal companions.

**The Whale Ancestor.** Spirit of deep waters, memory, truth and storms. It rules the Shattered Ice Sea and the migrating whales: a colossal whale of dark water and stars, its scars glowing like aurora. Slow to anger, but once roused, storms and bad currents follow liars and oathbreakers.

**The Stone-Mother.** Spirit of mountains, endurance and craft. It rules the Ashen Highlands and all the stone beneath the clans' feet. It speaks in tremors and echoes, and sometimes appears as a stone-skinned woman. It is offended by wasteful digging and needless harm to the land.

**The River-of-Mists.** Spirit of boundaries, journeys between worlds, and secrets. It rules the Mist-Bound Marshes, river mouths and fog banks: a long, winding shape of mist and drifting lights. Tricky but not cruel, it loves bargains, crossroads and choices.

**The Fallen Star-Spirit.** Spirit of change, curiosity and dangerous power. Long ago a star fell where the Ember-Heart Crater now lies, and its spirit is said to dwell there still: a figure of molten metal and light, always cracking and reforming. Its gifts are wild and unstable.

**The Ancestor Host.** Not one spirit but a great chorus of the honored dead, strongest at burial grounds and in the marshes. Some are proud, some bitter, some kind. Together they demand respect for tradition and proper ritual.

**The World-Tree.** Some say the pattern of all things is held by a great tree, or a great river, at the heart of the world. No one living has found it. It speaks, if at all, only in shared dreams and omens.

Shamans also speak, rarely and quietly, of something cold that sleeps under the ice of the Shattered Ice Sea, bound there by pacts older than any clan.

## Clan Guardians

Each clan is tied to one creature, and that creature's spirit is the clan's **Guardian**. It isn't any single wolf or seal, but the spirit of all of them, and it chose its clan long ago. It is a smaller cousin of the great spirits, and answers to one of them.

- **It guards** the clan's camps and hunting grounds, sends omens through its animals, and helps the spirit-talker in rituals.
- **When clan members die,** it guides them to the Ancestor Host.
- **It is protective but not soft.** It rewards loyalty and courage. If the clan breaks its taboos, it pulls back its protection: bad hunts, sickness, animals turning hostile.

| Clan | Guardian | Answers to | Creature taboo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wolf | the Pack-Beyond-the-Trees | Great Wolf | Never kill a wolf or eat wolf meat |
| Raven | the Finder | River-of-Mists | Always leave part of every kill for the ravens |
| Seal | the Watcher in the Swell | Whale Ancestor | Never kill a seal; clan-skins come only from seals the sea gives back |
| Owl | the Unblinking | Ancestor Host | Never kill an owl, and never imitate its call |
| Auroch | Firm-Foot | Stone-Mother | Never kill a herd's lead bull, and never leave horns on the plain |
| Boar | Ember-Tusk | Fallen Star-Spirit | Never run from a charging boar |
| Reindeer | the First Doe | Whale Ancestor | Never kill the lead doe; lay reindeer bones facing the herd's path |

Each clan page describes its Guardian more fully.

## The three souls

Everyone has three souls:

- **The name-soul:** who you are, carried in your name.
- **The clan-soul:** the part of you that belongs to your Guardian.
- **The world-soul:** the part of you that belongs to the world itself.

Losing your clan-soul means losing your place among the living and the dead.
