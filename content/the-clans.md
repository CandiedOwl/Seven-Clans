There are **seven clans**, one for each clan-creature: Wolf, Raven, Seal, Owl, Auroch, Boar and Reindeer. A clan is a family and a spiritual bond at once. Each is watched over by the Guardian of its creature ([Clan Guardians](@/spirits-of-the-world.md#clan-guardians)).

## How a clan is led

Every clan has some version of these roles, though each calls them something different.

- **The chief** speaks for the clan in matters of territory, trade and war. Chiefs are chosen for judgment and success, not just bloodline. They can't openly defy a major taboo or ignore the spirit-talker's omens without losing the clan's loyalty.
- **The spirit-talker** interprets omens, leads rites, handles curses and taboos, and deals with the spirits. A spirit-talker can stop a hunt if the omens are bad, but is expected to serve the clan, not rule it.
- **The elders** carry memory: old treaties, old feuds, the way things were done. Together they can overrule a chief. The real politics happens here, in quiet talk around a low fire.
- **The war leader or hunt leader** organizes hunts, patrols and training, and takes command in a fight.
- **Hearth-mothers and hearth-fathers** head the large families. They decide who eats, who shares which tent, and how small quarrels end. Most of daily life runs through them.
- **Storytellers** keep the clan's memory: origins, heroes, prophecies, and who owes whom. Which stories they tell loudly can steer a whole clan.
- **Young people** gather in bands: Auroch war bands, Boar raiding groups, Wolf and Reindeer hunting circles, and Raven and Seal trading and scouting crews.

In short: the chief decides *where we go and who our friends are*. The spirit-talker decides *what the spirits want*. The elders remember *what happened last time*. The hunt leader decides *who stands where*. The hearth-leaders decide *whether you get a bowl of stew tonight*.

## Who gets along with whom

- **Natural allies:** Wolf and Reindeer · Wolf and Owl · Reindeer and Seal · Auroch and Boar · Raven and Seal · Raven and Owl · Owl and Reindeer
- **Old feuds:** Wolf and Boar · Wolf and Raven · Raven and Auroch · Boar and Owl · Boar and Reindeer · Seal and Auroch
- **Tense, but they need each other:** Wolf and Auroch (forest meat for plains goods, but always arguing over boundaries). Everyone and Owl (respected for spirit-knowledge, feared for curses). Everyone inland and Seal (Seal control sea-goods and shell wealth).

Clans don't always agree about each other. Boar think of Seal as friends to raid alongside; Seal remember the coastal camps Boar have raided. Wolf call Raven their enemies; Raven just find Wolf too solemn. Each clan page lists what that clan says about the others.

Any two people from different clans have at least one old story about each other's people. Any gathering of clans is charged with them.
