**Home:** the Long Grass, south of the Wolfwood ([The Land](@/the-land.md)).

The Auroch live on open grasslands and in wide river valleys, following wild cattle and the great herds. They are bold, proud and honor-bound. Courage in the hunt and in battle is their highest virtue. They respect strength of body and of spirit, though they often mistake subtlety for weakness. Feasts, wrestling and contests are part of every gathering.

The Auroch see the world in **lines and boundaries**: who may graze where, whose river, whose hunting ground. That leads to clashes, but also to a fierce duty to defend what they claim.

## Their Guardian: Firm-Foot

A black bull taller than a man at the shoulder, with horns like the curve of the horizon. Dust rises where it stands, even in rain. It answers to the **Stone-Mother**. It walks the clan's boundary with them every season, and it watches every Challenge Circle.

**Taboo:** never kill the lead bull of a herd, and never leave an auroch's horns on the plain.

## Leaders

- The chief is the **Horn-Chief**. The current Horn-Chief is **Danek Stonehorn**: honorable, and suspicious of omens and "spirit excuses."
- The **war leader** is strong and formal, and commands the clan's war bands.
- The spirit-talker is respected, but if they clash with the Horn-Chief, lines are drawn.

## Traditions

- **Line-Walking:** each season the whole clan walks the rough boundary of its land, singing a litany of past battles and treaties.
- **The Horn Toast:** feasts begin by raising carved horn cups toward the horizon and asking the Auroch spirit for strength and "firm feet." Spilling during the toast is deeply shameful.
- **The Challenge Circle:** bare ground ringed with stones in every camp. Disputes are settled inside by wrestling or spear trials, watched closely by the elders.

## Rites of passage

- **First Stand in the Storm:** a young person stands on a hill in a storm with a wooden spear, feet planted, forbidden to move more than a few paces.
- **The Horn Mark** (adulthood): after a successful herd defense or a great hunt, they receive a horn tip on a cord, carved with the symbol of their deed.
- **The Blood and Dust Oath** (joining a war band): mix a drop of blood with dirt from the Challenge Circle, smear it on the brow, and swear to defend the clan's land "until the dust drinks us."

## Families

- **The Stonehorn Lodge:** the main ruling family across many camps. Pragmatic and proud. Horn-Chief **Danek Stonehorn** is a Stonehorn.
- **The Red-Mane Band:** a semi-independent war band known for red-dyed hair and braids. They border on reckless and often ride with Boar raiders. Their captain, **Mera Red-Mane**, is magnetic.
- **The Grass-Whisper Hearth:** herders and quiet trackers who understand the plains. Less warlike, more spiritual. They are the first to notice when the herds behave strangely.

## What the Auroch say about others

- **Boar:** they love glory and a good fight, as we do.
- **Wolf and Reindeer:** too cautious.
- **Seal:** we don't trust "boat folk."
- **Raven:** cattle thieves, who guide our enemies through our lands.
