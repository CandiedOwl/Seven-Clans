**[Clan mark](@/customs-and-beliefs.md#the-clan-mark).** **The hoofprint ring:** a circle of paired cloven hoofprints with no solid line, open at the bottom where the tracks trail away. It's tattooed around the wrist or ankle, and stitched into clothing and tent hides: an open ring for a people who never stay put.

**Home:** the White Reach, the tundra and taiga of the north-east ([The Land](@/the-land.md)).

The Reindeer range across the tundra, the taiga and the far-north forests, following the herds and the seasons. They are patient, methodical and season-wise. Their myths are full of long journeys and slow, certain change, and their sense of time is different: "wait for the third snow" is an ordinary instruction.

Family bonds are strong, and **hospitality is sacred**. Turning away a traveler in a blizzard is one of their gravest taboos.

## Their Guardian: the First Doe

The spirit of the lead doe who walked the first migration: a white doe whose antlers trail aurora light, and whose tracks fill with snow at once. It answers to the **Whale Ancestor**. It has no fixed seat; it walks with the herd. It is felt most at the small shrines of the Long Step and on the ridge of the Aurora Trial.

**Taboo:** never kill the lead doe of a herd, since the Guardian may be walking in her. Never leave reindeer bones scattered; they are laid facing the way the herd will walk.

## Leaders

- The chief is the **Trail-Master** or **Lead-Antler**.
- Because the clan is spread across several moving camps, decisions are made in council with many hearth-leaders.
- The spirit-talker reads the auroras and the migrations, almost like a chief of weather.

## Traditions

- **Sky Counting:** stars and auroras are tracked every night. Certain patterns mark when to move camp or expect a storm.
- **The Sharing of Trails:** remembered routes are recited aloud around the fire. Children learn the paths by listening, as if they were songs.
- **The Winter Guest Rule:** anyone sheltered in a Reindeer camp during a blizzard is "winter-kin" until the thaw, with rights and duties on both sides.

## Rites of passage

- **First Lead of the Herd:** a child guides a small group of reindeer from one grazing ground to another without losing any.
- **The Long Step** (adulthood): a solo journey between two distant seasonal camps, without being seen by other clans. On return, they place a stone or carved antler in their own small shrine.
- **The Aurora Trial** (for spiritual roles): stand alone on a cold ridge facing the lights, read their movement as a message, and bring it back. How the elders receive it decides your path.

## Families

- **The Snowtrace Hearth:** experts at reading tracks and ice. **Galin Snowtrace** is a seasoned pathfinder.
- **The Frostveil Camp:** known for beautiful beadwork and cloaks. They are usually the first Reindeer that outsiders meet at the southern edges, and they handle trade and diplomacy.
- **The Antlers-in-Mist Band:** a fast group that scouts ahead of the main herds. Their leader, **Risa Antlers-in-Mist**, is practical and wary of strange omens.

## What the Reindeer say about others

- **Wolf, Owl and Seal:** they care about the big patterns: weather, spirits, migrations.
- **Raven:** they stir trouble where there was none.
- **Auroch:** their boundary arguments make no sense. Land must be followed, not owned.
- **Boar:** they trample the ground and scare the herds. We remember the wintering sites they ruined.
