A short map of how things fit together: who you belong to, who leads, how the clans deal with each other, and the rules everyone lives by. Each part links to a page with more.

## The shape of things

There are no kings, no cities and no gods who hand down laws. There are **people**, gathered in **seven clans**, living among **spirits**. Everything that matters happens between those three: a hunt is a bargain with the spirit of the herd, a feud is an old story retold, and a broken taboo is a crack in the world.

The land is cold, wide and old. Eight regions are joined by one great river, the Mistwater, which runs from the highlands to the marshes and the sea ([The Land](@/the-land.md)).

## Where you belong

Everyone sits inside rings of belonging, from the fire you sleep beside out to the spirits that watch your clan.

| Ring | What it is | Who leads it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hearth** | The people you eat, sleep and travel with: one tent or longhouse fire. | A hearth-mother or hearth-father |
| **Family** | A named kin-group, such as the Hushfern Fire, the Foamtrail House or the Mudroot Cluster. Some are blood kin; some, like the Grey-Feather Circle, are bound by a calling. | Its matriarch, patriarch or circle |
| **Band** | A crew of young adults: a war band, raiding group, hunting circle or trading crew. Bands have their own leaders, and often their own opinions. | The band's captain |
| **Clan** | Everyone bound to the same creature and its Guardian. A clan may be spread across many camps. | The chief, spirit-talker and elders |
| **Guardian** | The spirit of the clan's creature, which watches over the clan, living and dead. | — |
| **Great spirit** | The greater spirit each Guardian answers to, such as the Great Wolf or the Whale Ancestor. | — |

Your **clan mark** and **clan-skin** show the world which rings you belong to ([the clan mark](@/customs-and-beliefs.md#the-clan-mark)). Your **clan-soul**, one of your three souls, belongs to your Guardian ([the three souls](@/spirits-of-the-world.md#the-three-souls)).

## Who leads a clan

Every clan has the same handful of roles, though each names them differently. The full picture is in [How a clan is led](@/the-clans.md#how-a-clan-is-led).

| Clan | Chief | Spirit-talker | Who else holds power |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Wolf](@/people-of-the-wolf.md) | Pack-Leader | Forest-Speaker | The elders of the Old Pack; the hunt leader is nearly the chief's equal |
| [Raven](@/people-of-the-raven.md) | First Voice, or Speaker of Feathers | Often the chief's rival | Storytellers and gossip-keepers |
| [Seal](@/people-of-the-seal.md) | Harbor-Chief, or Boat-Mother or Boat-Father | Deep-Listener | The Storm-Leader, who leads sea hunts and defense |
| [Owl](@/people-of-the-owl.md) | A quiet chief who rules by consensus | Seers, at the center of everything | The Grey-Feather Circle |
| [Auroch](@/people-of-the-auroch.md) | Horn-Chief | Respected, until they clash with the Horn-Chief | A strong, formal war leader and the war bands |
| [Boar](@/people-of-the-boar.md) | Big Tusks, won by challenge | Heard only when times are clearly cursed | The chief is usually the war leader too |
| [Reindeer](@/people-of-the-reindeer.md) | Trail-Master, or Lead-Antler | Reads the auroras and migrations | The council of hearth-leaders from every moving camp |

A rule of thumb: the chief decides *where we go*, the spirit-talker decides *what the spirits want*, the elders remember *what happened last time*, and the hearth-leaders decide *whether you eat tonight*.

## Between the clans

No one rules over all seven clans. When they must decide something together, they **hold a council**.

- **Councils** usually meet at the **Steam-Blood Springs** in the Ashen Highlands, which is neutral ground. A council can be called by a chief, or by a spirit-talker when the omens demand it.
- **The Peace-Singer**, a Raven of the Riddlesong Circle, moderates councils and works to keep feuds from becoming wars.
- **Fire-Speech** is the shared tongue of councils and trade ([Languages](@/making-a-character.md#languages)).
- **Trade** is barter: furs, flint, amber, meat, and the **shell wealth** that the Seal control. A favor owed is as good as goods, and Raven remember every one.
- **Alliances and feuds** are old, and every clan tells them its own way ([Who gets along with whom](@/the-clans.md#who-gets-along-with-whom)).

## The rules everyone lives by

There is no written law. Order is kept by **taboo**, **story** and **fire**.

- **Taboos** hold the world together. Each clan keeps its creature's taboos, and everyone keeps the shared ones: don't take more than you need, don't kill certain animals, don't disturb certain stones, trees and bones. Breaking a taboo isn't wickedness but **danger**, and it must be atoned for with a ritual, an offering or a quest ([Taboos and balance](@/customs-and-beliefs.md#taboos-and-balance)).
- **Story-Law:** what is told is true. Names hold power, stories gain weight with every telling, and what is never spoken fades ([Story-Law](@/customs-and-beliefs.md#story-law-what-is-told-is-true)).
- **Fire** is the line between the human world and the wild. Sharing fire is sharing life; putting out someone's fire is close to an attack ([Fire and the edge of the dark](@/customs-and-beliefs.md#fire-and-the-edge-of-the-dark)).
- **Disputes** go to the hearth-leaders first, then the elders, then the chief. Most never get past the first fire.
- **The worst punishments** are exile, with the clan mark cut or burned away, and death. Wearing a false clan mark can earn either.

## Outside the clans

- **The clanless** have no clan and no Guardian. They are freer and harder for spirits to read, but unprotected and often mistrusted.
- **The exiled** carry a scar where their mark used to be, and are treated as walking curses.
- **Spirit-twisters** use spirit power in ways every clan calls wrong. "Twister" is the heaviest accusation there is, and sometimes a political one ([Spirit-twisters](@/customs-and-beliefs.md#spirit-twisters)).
