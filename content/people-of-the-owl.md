**Home:** the Whispering Ravines, where the Wolfwood climbs into the Ashen Highlands ([The Land](@/the-land.md)).

The Owl live near ravines, cliffs and deep groves: places that feel close to the spirit world. They are secretive and inward-looking. Other clans find them eerie but necessary. When there's a curse, an omen or a strange dream, you go to the Owl.

They keep bone libraries, knotted-cord records and carved totems that hold their lore. Their taboos are intricate: what may be spoken, when fires may be lit, which foods to avoid in certain moons. Their spirit-talkers travel in dreams.

## Their Guardian: the Unblinking

It watches through every owl and never closes its eyes. It answers to the **Ancestor Host**. It is said to read every carved bone in the **Bone Libraries**. It appears as a great grey owl with a pale face like a mask, and sometimes the faces of the dead flicker behind it.

**Taboo:** never kill an owl, and never imitate an owl's call. It belongs to the dead.

## Leaders

- The chief is quiet and rules by consensus, often overshadowed by the **Grey-Feather Circle** of elders and spirit-talkers.
- Spirit-talkers and seers are at the center of Owl life. Owl politics is subtle and mystical.

## Traditions

- **Bone Libraries:** collections of carved bones and skulls holding stories, visions and taboos. Only initiates can read them all.
- **The Whisper Circle:** every new moon, no one speaks above a murmur. Dreams from the past month are shared and interpreted.
- **Night Masking:** special masks let spirits speak through "faces without names." Masks are never touched casually.

## Rites of passage

- **The Silent Watch:** a child stays awake all night alone on a ledge, tree or hilltop, and may not call out unless in real danger. They learn "how the dark moves."
- **Owl's Gaze** (adulthood): face-paint or a tattoo around the eyes, given in trance. The initiate must keep their eyes open as long as they can.
- **The Veil Walk** (for spirit-walkers): led blindfolded through dangerous ground, guided only by chants and drums, until the blindfold is lifted at a place of power.

## Families

- **The Grey-Feather Circle:** the council of wise ones, not all blood kin. **Elder Maeri Grey-Feather** looks frail, but her readings of omens carry weight with every clan.
- **The Mothwing Line:** a bloodline known for powerful, troubling visions. Some go mad, or wander into the forest and never return. **Tal Mothwing** is a young seer.
- **The Moss-Perch Hearth:** the practical branch of herbalists, midwives and bone-setters, linking the mystical and the everyday.

**Half-Ear**, an old Owl ancestor-listener, lives by the Ancestors' Crossing in the marshes. He is deaf in one ear from too many spirit-whispers, and often talks with companions no one else can see.

## What the Owl say about others

- **Wolf and Raven:** good exchanges of lore and signs.
- **Reindeer:** they care about balance with the seasons.
- **Seal:** distant and unfamiliar.
- **Auroch:** they call our warnings superstition, until it's too late.
- **Boar:** they mock our ways, and sometimes attack. Owl curses are whispered in return.
