**[Clan mark](@/customs-and-beliefs.md#the-clan-mark).** **Three notches:** a ring split into three arcs, each gap marked by a small triangle pointing inward. It's quick to cut with a knife, so it's everywhere: tattooed on the back of the hand, notched into arrow shafts, carved on the common totem. After the Pack's Claim, a hunter's own pack mark is cut beside it.

**Home:** the heart of the Deep Wolfwood ([The Land](@/the-land.md)).

The Wolf live deep in the great forest. They are quiet, pragmatic hunters, tied to the rhythms of the trees. Their hunting taboos are strict: no waste, no killing for sport, and special rituals for wolves, bears and great stags. Among the Wolf, *who brings meat* matters more than who your parents are.

They are wary of outsiders, but fair once trust is earned. Storytelling around the fire and shared hunts are the heart of their life.

## Their Guardian: the Pack-Beyond-the-Trees

The spirit of every wolf that has ever run with the clan. It answers to the **Great Wolf of the Deep Forest**. Its seat is the clan's **Wolf Stone**, where each new hunter presses a thumbprint of their own blood. It is never seen whole: only eyes, a flank or a tail at the edge of the firelight.

**Taboo:** never kill a wolf or eat wolf meat.

## Leaders

- **Pack-Leader Hald** of the River-Den rose as a hunt leader, not by birth. He is calm and outward-looking, and trusts trade and alliances.
- **Forest-Speaker Ashen-Bark** is the clan's spirit-talker: old, scarred, blind in one eye, with carved wooden wolves on her belt. Dry humor, sharp tongue. Her young apprentice is **Hare-Foot**.
- The elders are called **the Old Pack**. The hunt leader is almost as important as the chief.

## Traditions

- **Pack Sharing:** after a big hunt, the best cuts go first to elders and children, then outward. Refusing shared meat is rejecting the pack.
- **Silent Walks:** before an important decision, hunters walk the forest alone at dawn without speaking, until they hear a sign (a birdcall, a branch cracking, a distant howl). That is the forest's answer.
- **The Night of Listening:** every winter, all fires are dimmed and no stories are told. Everyone sits in silence, listening for wolves and spirits. Any sound heard that night is an omen for the year.

## Rites of passage

- **First Blood Hunt:** a young hunter tracks and brings down small game alone, then presses a bloody thumbprint on the Wolf Stone and vows never to kill wastefully.
- **The Pack's Claim** (adulthood): three nights alone in the forest with only a knife and a bow. On return, they carve their personal mark into the common totem pole. That **pack mark** goes on their tools and arrows.
- **The Last Howl** (death): the clan carries a hunter's body or ashes to a high hill and howls three times. If wolves answer, the dead have "found the pack beyond the trees."

## Families

- **The Hushfern Fire:** a large family known for soft voices and calm leadership. Many of the best trackers come from them. Their matriarch, **Anka Hushfern**, trusts omens but never panics.
- **The Flintfangs:** a hot-tempered family, "wolves too quick to bite," with a long history of clashes with Boar. **Terek Flintfang** is young and has something to prove.
- **The River-Den:** camps near the big river and handles trade with Seal and Raven. Their camp is where other clans most often meet the Wolf. **Hald**, the Pack-Leader, is River-Den.

## What the Wolf say about others

- **Reindeer and Owl:** friends. They respect the spirits and the seasons.
- **Seal and Auroch:** different, but we need their trade.
- **Raven:** untrustworthy. They stir up trouble.
- **Boar:** reckless and destructive in the forest. They break our hunting taboos.
