**Home:** the western coast, from the Shattered Ice Sea south toward the marshes ([The Land](@/the-land.md)).

The Seal live along coasts and islands, on shingle beaches and low sea cliffs. They are shaped by harsh seas and sudden storms. Drowning is always close, so cooperation is survival, and the Seal are communal and protective. One of their strongest taboos: **anger stays on shore.** Land quarrels are never carried onto the water.

They revere whale and seal spirits, and they are masters of currents and tides. Their songs are powerful, sung as charms for safe passage and good hunting.

## Their Guardian: the Watcher in the Swell

A huge grey seal whose spotted coat holds the patterns of the stars. It answers to the **Whale Ancestor**. Its seat is the **marked rock** of the Breaking-Ice Swim.

**Taboo:** never kill a seal. Seal clan-skins come only from seals the sea gives back, found dead on the shore. To wear hide from a *killed* seal is shocking.

## Leaders

- The chief is called the **Harbor-Chief**, or **Boat-Mother** or **Boat-Father**.
- The war leader is the **Storm-Leader**, who leads sea hunts and defense.
- The spirit-talker is the **Deep-Listener**: highly respected, but kept in check by the practical boat leaders.

## Traditions

- **Net Casting Songs:** work chants that are also protective charms. Singing off-rhythm brings bad luck and tangled nets.
- **The Safe Harbor Law:** any boat in distress must be helped, whatever its clan. Debts can be settled later. Ignore a drowning person and the sea will claim your own.
- **The Breaking-Ice Swim:** on the first day of the spring thaw, young people plunge into the frozen shallows and race to touch the marked rock. It "wakes the blood" after winter.

## Rites of passage

- **First Whale Shadow:** the first time a child sees the shadow of a great sea creature beneath their boat, an elder traces a whale on their back in charcoal: "*Now the Deep sees you.*"
- **The Oar's Burden** (adulthood): steer a boat through dangerous currents or a storm. Keep it on course and you earn your own oar, carved with seals and your clan mark.
- **The Salt-Trance** (for spirit-walkers): fast, drink brine and herbs, and lie on a tide-flat as the tide comes in and goes out. The visions shape your path.

## Families

- **The Foamtrail House:** the leading boat-builders. Their boats are named after kinds of waves. **Olla Foamtrail**, their practical matron, can commission or refuse a boat, which gives her great influence. **Brenna of the Broken Harpoon**, a young hunt-leader, is Foamtrail.
- **The Deepcallers:** spirit-talkers who "sing to the whales." Some say they can warn of storms. **Hek Deepcaller** was scarred by a storm long ago. **Taaruq**, the Whale-Speaker of the Whale-Bone Village, is a Deepcaller.
- **The Stone-Stack Camp:** a small group that marks safe channels with stacks of stones visible at low tide. They guide inland traders.

The **Whale-Bone Village** on the Shattered Ice Sea is a Seal village. Its people swear oaths by the tides and believe lies cause storms.

## What the Seal say about others

- **Raven:** trade partners and good gossip.
- **Reindeer:** good seasonal trade in meat, hides and tools.
- **Wolf:** forest folk, naïve about the sea.
- **Owl:** respected, but eerie.
- **Boar and Auroch:** they raid our coastal camps. We haven't forgotten the stolen boats.
