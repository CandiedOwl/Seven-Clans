**Home:** the crossings along the Mistwater, the great river ([The Land](@/the-land.md)).

The Raven range along forest edges, rocky outcrops and river crossings: the places where clans meet, trade and clash. They live by their wits: trading, scavenging, scouting and listening. They love riddles and wordplay. What outsiders call lying, Raven call *skillful speaking*.

They keep the oral histories of old taboos and broken oaths, and they know that a secret is worth more than a sack of flint. Their spirit-talkers deal in omens and trickster spirits.

## Their Guardian: the Finder

The spirit of every raven that ever stole a scrap or a secret. It answers to the **River-of-Mists**. Its seat is the lone tree or rocky outcrop of the **Black Feather Vigil**. It looks like a raven too large for its branch, always holding something in its beak.

**Taboo:** always leave part of every kill for the ravens.

## Leaders

- The chief is called the **First Voice** or **Speaker of Feathers**.
- The spirit-talker is often the chief's rival as much as their advisor.
- Storytellers and gossip-keepers have great power. The elder circle is loose and political.

## Traditions

- **Three-Sided Stories:** tales with three endings. Listeners choose which ending to believe. It teaches children to think about motives and hidden truths.
- **The Feast of Found Things:** every autumn, everyone brings something "found" (a lost tool, a secret, an odd bone, a rumor) to trade, with boasting and wagers.
- **The Cloaked Name:** most adults take a second name that only their closest family knows. Names known by too many are easier for spirits to twist.

## Rites of passage

- **The First Lie:** a child tricks an adult, harmlessly, then confesses before sunset. If no one was hurt, the adult must praise the lie's cleverness.
- **Raven's Wing Trial** (adulthood): carry a secret message between two distant camps without being caught, bribed or tricked into revealing it.
- **The Black Feather Vigil** (for spirit-talkers): a night alone on a lone tree or outcrop. If a raven lands within reach, the spirits "see" you.

## Families

- **The Riddlesong Circle:** singers, messengers and negotiators. Their leader, **Sari Riddlesong**, remembers every slight and favor and collects debts relentlessly, though never with violence. **Red-Reed** the Peace-Singer, who keeps the clans from open war, is of this circle.
- **The Bone-Tally Line:** scavengers and relic-traders who keep strings of bones as ledgers. **Jarn Bone-Tally** always has "just one more piece of information… for a price."
- **The Creekperchers:** camp on stony riverbanks and run ferries and toll crossings. Nobody is ever quite sure their tolls are fair. **Wenna Creekpercher** runs their ferry on the big river.

## What the Raven say about others

- **Seal:** trade partners and fellow gossips.
- **Owl:** they understand the value of a secret.
- **Wolf and Reindeer:** too solemn and too rigid.
- **Auroch and Boar:** they call us cowards and thieves.
